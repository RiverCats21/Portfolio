Portfolio Overview

This website presents a curated portfolio of academic and applied work completed as part of the AI in Construction and Facilities Management course. The projects and analyses showcased here focus on practical applications of artificial intelligence, structured decision-making, and data analytics within construction and facilities engineering.

Rather than treating AI as a purely theoretical concept, this portfolio emphasizes real-world use cases such as bid evaluation, risk analysis, lifecycle planning, and facilities maintenance strategy.
